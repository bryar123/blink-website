What this website collects

This website does not ask you to create an account and does not use advertising or tracking cookies.

  • Project brief. The brief form does not send anything to our servers. It prepares a message that you choose to send yourself through WhatsApp or your email app. Once you send it, we receive your name, email address and the details you wrote, and we use them only to reply about your project.
  • Your settings. Your language, light or dark mode and motion setting are saved in your own browser so the site remembers them. They never leave your device.
  • Visit statistics. We may use Cloudflare Web Analytics, which counts visits without cookies and without identifying you.
  • Hosting. The site is hosted by Cloudflare, which processes basic technical data such as IP addresses to deliver and protect the site.
